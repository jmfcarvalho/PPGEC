ACADEMIC REGULATIONS

Study Plan, Credits & Graduation Milestones

Master's (M.Sc.)

18 Credits + Seminar

  • • CIV 797 Seminar: 2nd semester.
  • • CIV 799 Research: From 2nd sem.
  • • Duration: 24 months (Max 36).
  • • Article: 1 submitted paper.
Regular time: 4 semesters.
Doctorate (Ph.D.)

12 Credits (+ M.Sc.)

  • • CIV 797 Seminar: 3rd semester.
  • • Qualification: By 6th semester.
  • • Teaching Internship: Mandatory.
  • • Duration: 48 months (Max 60).
Regular time: 8 semesters.
Mandatory Rules

English Proficiency

  • • Submit proficiency proof in Study Plan by end of 2nd semester.
  • • CRA Requirement: $\ge 75\%$ overall academic performance index.
  • • Failure to register CIV 799 causes compulsory dismissal!

Mandatory Incoming Student Checklist

Immediate Administrative Steps

  1. Institutional Email: Activate @ufv.br account and update AcademicoPG profile.
  2. Lattes Curriculum: Update Lattes CV and send direct link to PPGEC secretariat.
  3. ORCID & Social Networks: Send ORCID ID, LinkedIn, and ResearchGate URLs.
  4. Website Photo: Submit professional photo for student page (or default avatar).
  5. Self-Assessment Survey: Fill out PPGEC institutional self-evaluation form when requested.
