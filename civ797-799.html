BEST PRACTICES

Visual & Technical Slide Rules

Typography & Numbers

Always insert slide numbers. Use font size $\ge 18\text{pt}$ for body text. Keep titles concise.

Color Contrast

Choose title/text colors carefully. Avoid high-contrast bright red or pure yellow text for readability.

Content Synthesis

Avoid text paragraphs — use telegraphic text. Flowcharts and figures are far better than explanatory text.

VISUAL METHODOLOGY

Diagrams & Flowcharts in Defense Slides

Why Flowcharts Over Plain Text?

  • Cognitive Efficiency: Visual workflows allow examiners to grasp complex experimental steps instantaneously during a short talk.
  • Structured Logic: Clear directional connectors demonstrate exact procedural sequence and analytical dependencies.
  • Design Tip: Keep block shapes uniform, minimize text density inside nodes, and use color coding to group experimental phases.

VISUAL DESIGN GUIDELINES

Presenting Tabular Data Effectively

Cognitive Rationale

Tables designed for academic papers serve archival completeness, whereas slides require instant cognitive comprehension.

  • Avoid Screen Clutter: Unfiltered raw data forces viewers to read micro-text rather than listen to the speaker.
  • Distance Legibility: Font sizes under 18pt are completely illegible from the back row of an auditorium.
4 RULES FOR SLIDE TABLES
  • • 1. Filter Aggressively: Extract only key variables tied directly to the narrative.
  • • 2. Simplify Units: Group metrics with clear, consistent dimensional units.
  • • 3. Highlight Key Metrics: Use contrasting background fills or bolding for critical findings.
  • • 4. Maximize Spacing: Ensure generous cell padding and high-contrast typography.
INADEQUATE APPROACH (BRANCH Y=1)

Anti-Pattern: Raw Paper Table Copy-Paste

DO NOT DO THIS

Why This Fails

  • • Unreadable micro-fonts (<10pt) force audience squinting.
  • • Excessive background noise (too many rows/columns).
  • • Zero visual hierarchy or focus on key metrics.
  • • High cognitive fatigue for the evaluator.

CIV 799 — RESEARCH

Research Phase, Concomitant Enrollment & Bond Maintenance

Subject Transition

Research Execution

No traditional classroom load: Student duties consist purely of carrying out approved research activities under advisor supervision.

  • Evaluated semi-annually as Satisfactory (S) or Unsatisfactory (N).
  • No credit hours generated; does not impact overall GPA coefficient.
Marks transition from coursework phase into active thesis/dissertation development.
Program Link Safety

Preventing Dismissal

CIV 799 serves as the formal academic link maintaining active student enrollment status within PPGEC.

Mandatory Study Plan Action:

At least CIV 799 MUST be added to the Study Plan during course registration every single semester to avoid official program dismissal (desligamento).

Mandatory from 2nd sem (MSc) & 3rd sem (PhD) onwards.
Co-requisites & Milestones

Concomitant Registration

Board Composition & Defense Requirements

Prerequisites

Language & Publications

  • Language Options: Manuscripts may be written and defended in English, Portuguese, or Spanish.
  • M.Sc. Defense: Proof of at least 1 scientific article submitted.
  • Ph.D. Defense: Proof of 2nd distinct submitted article + similarity report.

Committee Rules

Examining Board Setup

  • • M.Sc. Defense: Minimum 3 members (at least 1 external to PPGEC).
  • • Ph.D. Qualification & Defense: Shared board requirements — minimum 5 members (at least 1 external to PPGEC and 1 external to UFV). More members may be included if necessary.
  • • Substitute Members (Suplentes): Mandatory indication of 2 substitute members, with at least 1 external to UFV.
Ensures full committee coverage in case of unexpected absences.
